## Competition-code license clarification — reviewed 2026-11-24

The Budget-Fit source is version 5 of Wilmer E. Henao's public [competition notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/verracodeguacas/gemma-4-agent-budget-fit-single-agent?scriptVersionId=355310852). The versioned page is direct evidence of the license declaration for this source bundle, even though the pulled `kernel-metadata.json` has no license field and the notebook source has no embedded license header. Preserve the author's attribution and Apache-2.0 license when redistributing the bundle or its derivatives. This is a provenance finding, not a legal determination.

The [competition rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemma-4-developer-agent/rules) also name **Open Source Apache 2.0** as the winner license type and require the winning submission and source code to be licensed accordingly, subject to the entrant's representation that they have the right to grant that license. The Foundational Rules' public-code clause additionally deems code shared in an associated public notebook/forum licensed under an OSI-approved license permitting commercial use. The official [OSI license list](https://opensource.org/licenses) includes Apache-2.0, and the [Apache Software Foundation identifies Apache License 2.0 as OSI-approved](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html). The notebook and its Kaggle `kernel-metadata.json` remain under ignored `data/local/research/license-audit-v5-notebook-2026-11-24/`; the metadata/source omission does not erase the rendered page-level declaration.
